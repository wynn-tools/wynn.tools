# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Wynncraft players — people who already use hppeng-wynn.github.io or WynnData, or who find the link from Discord or the Wynntils project. They know what Wynncraft is. They don't need to be sold on the game. They want to know: what is this, is it legit, when does it ship, and is there somewhere to follow progress.

Secondary: Wynncraft community members who contribute code, maintain builds, or curate content. They care that this is open-source and community-governed.

## Product Purpose

wynn.tools is a unified platform consolidating the Wynncraft community's existing tools (Builder, Map, Crafter, Atlas) into one site, adding a sharing layer so builds, lootruns, and info box overlays have permanent linkable homes instead of Discord blobs. It replaces hppeng-wynn.github.io as the community's primary tool hub.

Success: players switch their default builder bookmark from hppeng to wynn.tools on launch day. Community contributors recognise it as the canonical, maintained project.

## Brand Personality

Serious, precise, community.

Not a startup trying to impress investors. A tool built by players who actually play. It should feel like the work of someone who spent 400 hours in the Wynncraft endgame and decided to do this properly.

Tone: direct, no filler. Say what the thing does. Let the tools speak.

## Anti-references

- **hppeng-wynn.github.io and WynnData** — cluttered PHP-era layout, no visual coherence, feels like it was never designed
- **Crypto / NFT landing pages** — neon on black, blurry hero gradients, vague hype copy ("the future of X")
- **Generic SaaS landing pages** — feature-icon grids, "Ship faster" headlines, identical three-card rows, orbital glow effects
- **Fantasy RPG clichés** — parchment textures, ornate Celtic borders, pixel art or medieval fonts, anything that says "this is a game" more than "this is a tool"

## Design Principles

1. **Tools earn the respect, not the design.** The page exists to direct traffic and set expectations. It should not oversell. Let the tools prove themselves when they ship.
2. **Community signal over corporate.** Open-source, Wynntils-adjacent, Discord-native. The page should feel like it was made by someone with a GitHub account and 400 hours in-game, not a design agency.
3. **No preamble.** Every word must work. No "welcome to wynn.tools" openers, no restated headings, no section labels that repeat the content beneath them.
4. **Precision over personality.** When in doubt, do less. Tighter spacing beats decorative spacing. One font weight contrast beats two decorative elements.
5. **Version-aware from day one.** The core concept of the platform is that content gets stale. That philosophy should be visible even in the coming-soon page: specificity, dates, concrete feature descriptions over vague promises.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Reasonable color contrast for the dark theme. No motion-only communication. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
